How it works

Three steps. That's it.

  1. 1

    Copy the script

    Tap Copy in the box below. It is one line.

  2. 2

    Run it in your executor

    Paste it, press run. The Avatar Studio window opens.

  3. 3

    Type a name, tap Apply

    Search anyone in Copy Avatar and tap Apply avatar.

Changed your mind? Undo goes back one step and Restore brings back your own avatar.
